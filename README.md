# Second Brain (starter)

A folder that turns what you read into your own wiki. You collect; Claude reads, files and links.

## Build it together

1. Open this folder in the Code tab. Fill in `profile.md`: choose whether it follows **AI** or **your own field**.
2. Ask: `Set up git for this folder and save a first version.`
3. Capture three things you already have: `/capture <link>`, `/capture <file>`, or paste text after `/capture`.
4. `/ingest` the first one. Answer its two questions. Open the new pages in `wiki/`.
5. Ingest the other two, then ask it something: `/ask what do my sources say about <topic>?`
6. Open `.claude/skills/ask/SKILL.md` and change one thing to suit you. Try `/ask` again.
7. `/weekly` to finish: a summary, three quiz questions, and a saved version.

Not sure what to capture? For AI, try https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents. For your field, use a recent article, a PDF you keep meaning to read, or your notes from a meeting (with made up names).

## Next ideas

- `/quiz <topic>`: questions marked against your wiki.
- `/lint`: find broken links, gaps and claims that disagree.
- A morning `/today` email, or a password protected page that searches your wiki.
