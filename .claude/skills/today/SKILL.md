Time available: $ARGUMENTS (if empty, assume 15 minutes)

1. Read `profile.md`, the last ten entries in `wiki/log.md`, and the header of every file in `raw/`.
2. Reply with four short parts:
   - **Waiting:** raw files with `status: new`.
   - **Recently:** what was added or changed in the last week.
   - **Open:** any **Disagreement** marks or questions left in topic pages.
   - **Suggestion:** one thing to do that fits the time, with the exact command.
3. Do not change any file.
